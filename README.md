## Featured Projects

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <h3>GraamSehat</h3>
      <sub><b>Rural digital healthcare suite</b></sub>
      <p>Five connected apps (landing page, villager app, ASHA worker portal, admin dashboard, IVR diagnostics) synced through Firestore. Built to close the gap in rural clinical care delivery.</p>
      <p><b>Problem:</b> ASHA workers in low-connectivity areas need to screen, register, and track patients without a reliable network.</p>
      <p><b>Stack:</b> JavaScript, Firebase / Firestore, Dexie (IndexedDB), Vite, Android PWA wrappers</p>
      <p><a href="https://github.com/HAFIZ-HAASHIM/GraamSehat">Repository</a> · <a href="https://graam-sehat.vercel.app">Live demo</a></p>
    </td>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <h3>ThermoShift</h3>
      <sub><b>Heat-aware workforce scheduling</b></sub>
      <p>Decision-support system that generates heat-safe work and rest schedules for outdoor industrial sites, using a constraint-programming optimizer, live weather and WBGT modelling, and PDF schedule import.</p>
      <p><b>Problem:</b> Manual shift planning breaks down when heat spikes, while project deadlines stay fixed.</p>
      <p><b>Stack:</b> Python, Google OR-Tools (CP-SAT), FastAPI, React, TypeScript, Supabase</p>
      <p><a href="https://github.com/HAFIZ-HAASHIM/thermoshift">Repository</a> · <a href="https://thermoshift.vercel.app">Live demo</a></p>
    </td>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <h3>HarvestIQ</h3>
      <sub><b>AI crop-selling advisor for farmers</b></sub>
      <p>Analyzes historical mandi prices, forecasts short-term trends, and gives clear Sell or Wait recommendations with weather-risk insights and SMS alerts. Built at Sahyadri SahneX Protothon 2026.</p>
      <p><b>Problem:</b> Farmers often sell at the market's lowest point because they have no price forecasting.</p>
      <p><b>Stack:</b> JavaScript, Node.js, Express, Firebase, Twilio</p>
      <p><a href="https://github.com/HAFIZ-HAASHIM/HarvestIQ">Repository</a></p>
